RideDeck is a free, open-source standalone Android in-vehicle driving dashboard inspired by CarPlay and Android Auto. It runs on any compatible mounted Android phone, tablet or standalone head unit; it does not integrate with or project to CarPlay or Android Auto.

DAILY DRIVING
- Landscape dashboard with GPS speed, clock, battery level and phone temperature; large, high-contrast touch targets (64-84 dp) for quick glances and easy taps, plus an automatic day/night theme.
- Settings and text entry lock while moving (5 km/h and above), so the screen stays glanceable.

NAVIGATION (requires internet)
- OpenStreetMap-based map rendered with MapLibre/OpenFreeMap, vehicle routing via Valhalla "auto" costing, turn-by-turn voice guidance and recalculation when you leave the route.
- Saved places (Home, Work, favorites), waypoints, fuel stops along or near the route, speed-limit alerts and arrival time.
- Routing, place search, weather and speed-limit lookups contact public third-party services. Offline routing is unavailable. Previously downloaded map areas are kept, but new offline downloads are paused.

LANGUAGE, MEDIA AND PHONE
- Turkish or English app language via Android 13+ app-language settings; the TTS voice and route instructions switch without restarting the app. Unsupported phone languages fall back to English.
- Media-session controls for music apps, optional message readout, and an in-app phone with contacts, recents and a keypad. Some functions depend on Android permissions and the default dialer role, including hands-free/Bluetooth call audio.

TRIPS, SOS AND PRIVACY
- Local trip recording and history review; trips and settings stay on the device. No account and no RideDeck backend are required.
- Optional, experimental SOS: after a detected hard impact (5.0 g) while moving (30 km/h or above) and inactivity, a countdown can be followed by an attempted SMS with a location link to up to 3 contacts. It is not field-tested, does not guarantee delivery, and is not a substitute for emergency services or safety equipment.
- Map tiles, search text, route endpoints, approximate location for weather and fuel-stop queries are sent to third-party providers; those providers can see your IP address.

STATUS AND LIMITS
- v0.7.1 is a stable release, but the project is not a 1.0 product yet. Real-device and real-driving validation, real SMS/calls, Bluetooth hands-free audio, lock-screen/OEM behavior and crash-detection thresholds remain unverified.
- No live traffic, radar alerts, CarPlay or Android Auto integration. Android 8.0+ (API 26).

If screenshots are used in store listings, they are captured on an Android emulator using synthetic/simulated GPS, not real driving.

RideDeck can be distracting while driving: mount your phone or tablet securely, set up settings while parked, and follow traffic laws. Licensed under Apache 2.0.
