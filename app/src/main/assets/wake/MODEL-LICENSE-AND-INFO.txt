The English GigaSpeech Zipformer KWS model runs locally through sherpa-onnx.
The custom keyword file encodes “HEY RIDEDECK” and “HEY RIDE DECK” with the
model's BPE vocabulary. Wake-word and speech accuracy depends on microphone,
accent, noise, and device conditions.
